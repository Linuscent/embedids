<h2 id="product-features">Product Features</h2>
<h3 id="open-source-sdk-apache-20-licensed">Open-Source SDK (Apache 2.0 Licensed)</h3>
<p>One C11 library. You provide the buffers; it checks every sample against your rules.</p>
<ul class="chips">
<li>No heap allocation</li>
<li>Threshold rules</li>
<li>Custom algorithms</li>
<li>CMake package</li>
<li>Zephyr module</li>
</ul>

<section class="home-band home-why" aria-labelledby="why-embedids" data-reveal>
<h2 id="why-embedids">Why EmbedIDS?</h2>
<ol class="answers">
<li><span class="problem">Tight memory and power budgets</span><span class="answer">Fixed memory, no heap allocation</span></li>
<li><span class="problem">Unreliable connectivity</span><span class="answer">Detection runs offline, on the device</span></li>
<li><span class="problem">Fleet-scale costs</span><span class="answer">Open source, with no licence fees</span></li>
<li><span class="problem">Diverse hardware</span><span class="answer">Portable C11 core, Linux agent and Zephyr module</span></li>
</ol>
<div class="proof">
<a class="proof-card" href="case-studies/edgeml-runtime-security/"><span class="proof-title">Edge AI devices</span><span class="proof-text">Model tampering flagged in 1 s by a ~9 MB agent, in simulation</span><Icon name="right-arrow" class="proof-arrow" /></a>
<a class="proof-card" href="case-studies/zephyr-runtime-security/"><span class="proof-title">Zephyr RTOS</span><span class="proof-text">Every scripted attack flagged within 120 ms, in simulation</span><Icon name="right-arrow" class="proof-arrow" /></a>
</div>
